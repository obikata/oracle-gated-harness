# Ratchet log

Every failure that reached triage and could have been caught earlier becomes a
permanent guard. One line per guard. Newest first.

| Date | Failure seen | Guard added | Type |
|---|---|---|---|

Types: `test` (new oracle test), `hook` (enforcement), `claude_md_rule` (instruction),
`orchestrator` (run control), `zone` (zones.json change), `evaluator` (domain rule).
